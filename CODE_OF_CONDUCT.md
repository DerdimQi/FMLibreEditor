# Code of conduct

FMLibreEditor is made by people who play Football Manager in different countries, languages and
storefronts, and who know very different amounts about Rust, React or reverse engineering. Everyone
is welcome to ask, report, test and contribute, whatever they know.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, in issues, pull requests and Discussions. Assume good intent.
- Welcome questions, including the ones that look basic. Nobody is expected to know the codebase.
- Review the work, not the person. Say what you would change and why.
- Write in English where you can, so everyone can follow; a message in another language is fine
  too, and nobody is judged for their English.
- Credit the people whose findings you build on.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sharing cracked copies of the game or other pirated material, or asking for them.
- Spam, and repeatedly pushing a topic after the maintainer has asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

For something that has to stay private, such as harassment, use GitHub's **Report content** menu on
the comment, issue or pull request (the **⋯** button), which reaches GitHub's own moderators. For
anything else, mention the maintainer, @tozmoz, in the thread. The maintainer may edit or hide comments, lock threads, and block
people who do not follow this code.

This code of conduct takes its spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
